Principles

Black and white
Primary is black in light mode and white in dark. Color only means status.
Borders, not shadows
Surfaces are separated by 1 px hairlines. Only floating layers get a shadow.
Compact
34 px controls with 14 px text. The text fills the control, not the air.
Bold type
Manrope at 500, 700 and 800. Never thin.
Solid
No blur, glass, gradients or glow.

  return (
    <html lang="en">
      <body>
        {children}
        <Toaster />
      </body>
    </html>
  );
}
